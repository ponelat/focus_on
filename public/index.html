You went freelance so nobody could own you. Why let a subscription own your invoices? FocusOn tracks hours and generates invoices from files on your own disk. There's no account, no monthly bill, no company standing between you and your own timesheet.

The old deal

Hosted time trackers are good tools with a bad bargain buried in them. You feed them every hour you work, every client you have, every rate you charge, and they bill you what they want. One of the biggest names in the category was acquired in 2025; its new owner added usage fees on top of the seat price in 2026.

That's the deal. It does not get better.
